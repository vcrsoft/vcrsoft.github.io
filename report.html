Why This Structure Changes the Game

1. Apples-to-Apples Feature Breakdown

Normalizes conflicting vendor terminologies across equipment tiers, warranties, and maintenance commitments without arbitrary scoring. For instance, it directly pairs Proposal A's standard ACME RTU-125 units (capped at 11.2 EER) against Proposal B's high-efficiency Carrier units equipped with variable-speed indoor blower motors, helping you evaluate engineering specs side-by-side.

2. Exposing Hidden Price Differences & Scope Gaps

Unpacks the exact $14,400 price delta between Proposal A ($68,500) and Proposal B ($82,900). Rather than leaving you to guess why XYZ Climate Solutions costs more, the report highlights tangible additions: duct modifications, digital programmable thermostats, building management system integration, and extended 5-year parts / 2-year labor warranties versus Proposal A's baseline 1-year terms.

3. Operational Disruption & Scheduling Impact

Translates fine print into business realities. Proposal A restricts work to standard business hours over 2 days, which risks shutting down a restaurant during peak service hours and hurting revenue. Proposal B accounts for this operational risk by factoring in the flexibility to schedule work outside operating hours.

4. Hard-Hitting Questions for Vendors

Equips you with targeted questions to level the playing field before signing. By analyzing omissions in both source documents—such as Proposal B's missing exact EER efficiency ratings and Proposal A's lack of duct integration clarity—the tool forces contractors to close loopholes and clarify missing scope upfront.
