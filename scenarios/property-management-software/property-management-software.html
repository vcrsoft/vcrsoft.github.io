Why this scenario matters — not AI chatbots, real PMS ops

Most small PMs outgrow Excel, then hit Buildium/AppFolio pricing and QBO sync issues. Real pain is trust accounting integrity, owner reporting, and maintenance lifecycle with photos/receipts. This brief shows tradeoffs: stay and patch with VendorPM/Property Meld, switch to RentManager/DoorLoop, or re-quote AppFolio at 120 doors.

  • Trigger: Buildium price increase + 2-day month-end close
  • Current: Buildium 3yr + QBO + sheets, 30 WOs/week, photos in text threads
  • Must-haves: True trust accounting, reliable QBO sync, owner portal per-property P&L, WO lifecycle with photos + vendor billing, mobile field app
  • Constraints: <$250/mo all-in, 2-year history migration, hands-on onboarding, blackout Oct-Dec renewal season
  • Risks: Deposit/owner balance migration errors = lawsuit, double entry creeping back, vendors ignoring portal
  • Success in 6 months: Close in 4 hours not 2 days, owners self-serve, every WO has photo+receipt billed to right property, no double entry
