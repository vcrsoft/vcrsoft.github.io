Why this scenario matters — field adoption and callback costs

Home services companies outgrow Housecall Pro or Jobber for dispatch but lack a true CRM. Cross-sell across trades is invisible, $12k HVAC jobs are lost if leads aren't followed up in 10 minutes, and warranty callbacks eat margin because they aren't linked to the original job.

  • Trigger: 600 maintenance plans renewing at 62% and 1,100 open estimates with no stage tracking
  • Current: Housecall Pro + QBO + spreadsheets, 4,200 customers, 300 leads/mo, 25 installs/mo at $8k-$15k
  • Must-haves: Single customer view across 3 trades, HVAC install pipeline with financing and close reasons, callback ticket linked to original job with cost, maintenance renewal automation via text
  • Constraints: Under $400/mo for 5 seats, keep Housecall as dispatch, sync with Housecall and QBO without double entry, 2-year history migration, no go-live June-Aug
  • Risks: Techs won't use extra steps, callbacks not linked, Google Ads leads not auto-flowing
  • Success in 6 months: One record per customer, installs tracked to source, callbacks tied to cost, renewal rate 62% to 85%, cross-trade repeat up 20%
