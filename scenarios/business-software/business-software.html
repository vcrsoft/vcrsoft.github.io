Why this scenario matters — spreadsheet SCM breaks at 2 warehouses

QuickBooks + Sheets works until 2 warehouses, 120 SKUs, 12 suppliers. Then stockouts on top 20 SKUs, $30k dead stock, manual cycle counts weekly, and Shopify to ShipStation needs owner checking Sheets. Small business software starts near $5k/year but procurement strength and barcode simplicity determine adoption. Cloud dashboard matters for owner checking live stock from phone.
