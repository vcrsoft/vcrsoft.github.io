Why this scenario matters — occupied work and warranty risk

Strip mall roofs can't close tenants. Real decisions hinge on phased work, tenant protection, interior damage liability, deck rot change orders, and manufacturer NDL vs paper warranty. Prior cheap roofer and GC overrun history makes owner demand direct licensed roofer with references.

  • Trigger: Insurance non-renewal in 6 months + 2 tenants threatening rent withholding + 40% wet insulation
  • Current: 22-year-old TPO, leaks in 3 bays, brittle membrane, dentist $15k equipment at risk
  • Must-haves: Licensed roofer direct (not GC), 20-year manufacturer NDL, phased 4 sections max 2 bays at a time, no work dentist Tue-Thu days, nights/weekends preferred, daily cleanup
  • Constraints: $180k-$240k all-in including tear-off, ISO, 60-mil TPO, drains, parapet, permits Pearland, complete by July 31 before August storms
  • Risks: Interior damage liability, tenant business interruption claims, deck rot change-order abuse, paper vs manufacturer warranty
  • Success in 6 months: Roof replaced by July 31 zero closures, 20-year manufacturer NDL in hand, cost within budget including deck unit pricing, no tenant claims, insurance renewed
