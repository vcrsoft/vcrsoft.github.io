Why this scenario matters — theft, variance, and tablet chaos

Fast-casual tacos live or die in lunch rush 11-2, 60% of sales in 3 hours. Three tablets + paper kitchen = double entry and missed 86s. $8k food variance could be theft, waste, or recipe cost — Square can't tell. Real decision is payment rate vs true profit, KDS reliability vs lunch rush, and whether cooks will actually log waste.

  • Trigger: $8k food cost variance, staff comp theft, double-entered DoorDash/Uber, 38% labor unknown
  • Current: Square on iPads + DoorDash tablet + Uber tablet + paper kitchen, manual inventory Sunday counting boxes, 1,800 tickets/week, $14.50 avg
  • Must-haves: Unified delivery into one KDS Spanish UI, recipe-level depletion, live labor % with scheduling/clock-in, manager PIN for voids/comps, offline mode, per-location P&L after food cost
  • Constraints: Under $500/mo total both locations incl software + payments near 2.6%, hardware under $2k total, migrate July not SXSW/ACL, Spanish UI + hands-on training not YouTube
  • Risks: Staff turnover training churn, KDS down during lunch rush, inventory steps ignored like MarketMan, paying more for same problems
  • Success in 6 months: One dashboard both shops real profit after food/labor, variance $8k to <$1.5k, DoorDash/Uber in one KDS no tablets, labor 38% to 31%, owner 50 hrs not 75
