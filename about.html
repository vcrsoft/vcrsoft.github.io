About Proposal Compare by VCRSoft

Proposal Compare (ProposalCompare) is a purpose-built tool for objective comparison of complex business proposals, vendor bids, quotes, and RFP responses.

What it does

  • Upload 2 or more proposals (PDF)
  • Finds missing details, vague scope descriptions, excluded maintenance items, hidden costs and risk flags that standard reviews miss
  • Generates precise, hard-hitting questions to ask vendors to level the playing field
  • Provides a real feature-by-feature breakdown - apples-to-apples comparison without artificial scores or forced rankings
  • Report is $99 per comparison, no subscription or ongoing commitment
  • Uploaded proposals and session data are deleted promptly after comparison. We do not retain, sell, or train on your documents.

Who should use it

Built for procurement teams, operations managers, business owners, and anyone evaluating high-stakes vendor proposals:

  • Vendor bid comparison and evaluation
  • Business proposal comparison
  • Quote comparison for IT services, construction, managed services, SaaS, equipment, consulting
  • RFP response analysis and scoring
  • Complex purchase decision support

How it works

  1. Go to propcompare.pythonanywhere.com or vcrsoft.com
  2. Upload 2+ proposal PDFs
  3. Get your comparison report: missing items, scope gaps, feature matrix, vendor questions
