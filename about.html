About vcrsoft

vcrsoft is AI-native growth consulting for small and midsize businesses. Instead of a long engagement, you have one focused conversation (about 60 minutes) about how your business wins customers, delivers work, and grows — then you get a structured growth strategy report: a diagnosis, prioritized opportunities, a sequence, a 30-day plan, and measures of success. You review a draft and correct it before anything is final.

The consultation costs $49, paid upfront before the interview starts. All sales are final — no refunds for any reason. The interview runs at vcrsoft.pythonanywhere.com.

The Vcrsoft Way

Every consultation follows five steps (see the framework): understand the business; diagnose the growth problem; identify AI-enabled solutions; prioritize opportunities by value, feasibility, cost, risk, and time to benefit; and deliver an actionable strategy. Separately, the conversation moves through six business phases (see the six phases) — discovery lenses that serve the steps, not stages inside them.
