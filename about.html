About DecisionBrief by VCRSoft

DecisionBrief is a McKinsey-tiny tool for early decisions. A 4–8 minute anonymous chatbot interview crystallizes your situation into a clear, structured HTML page and a downloadable PDF artifact — before you buy, build, hire, or switch vendors.

$9 one-time per brief. No subscription. Anonymous by design.
No login, no account, no database. Session cookie only, server memory only, auto-discarded after 1 hour. We do not retain or train on your inputs. Stripe checkout verified via webhook.

What it does

  • No proposals to upload. You start via a scenario entry point like decisionbrief.pythonanywhere.com/app?scenario=choose-crm
  • Guided interview — 1 question per turn — covers trigger, current approach, volume, prior attempts, must-haves, constraints, risks, desired outcome. 6–10 turns.
  • Produces a structured Decision Brief: executive summary, decision, decision maker, situation, desired outcome, prior experience, priorities, constraints, options (including do-nothing), tradeoffs, risks & concerns, unknowns, decision criteria, next steps, recommendation
  • HTML and PDF artifact is $9 via Stripe — same artifact style you see embedded on report.html
  • Anonymous, private session discarded after completion. No training, no retention.
