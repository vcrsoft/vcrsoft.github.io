The VCRSoft differentiation

A consulting approach designed to understand the business before prescribing technology.

  1. Diagnosis before prescription

    The consultant investigates how the business actually works, including goals, economics, bottlenecks, and constraints, instead of immediately recommending chatbots and automation.

  2. A working hypothesis that gets tested

    It develops a tentative explanation for the growth problem, explores alternative explanations, and asks questions that can change the diagnosis.

  3. Specificity without a rigid questionnaire

    The interview uses the relevant industry's economics and operational patterns as discovery aids, while allowing the owner's answers to determine which issues deserve further investigation.

  4. A decision-ready deliverable

    The report connects evidence to recommendations, explains feasibility and risk, identifies what remains unknown, and gives the owner a sequence of actions and measures of success.

  5. The owner can challenge the result

    A draft is presented for correction and feedback before finalization. This helps make the report a collaborative business decision rather than an AI-generated pronouncement.
