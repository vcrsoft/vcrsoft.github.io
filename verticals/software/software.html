Why Evaluating Complex SaaS CRM Bids Matters

1. Platform Architecture & License Pooling

Scrutinizes multi-tenant vs single-tenant DB isolation, pooled storage (500GB vs 2TB), API call limits (50k vs 250k/day), and burst licensing that allows growth from 28 to 40 users without new seat purchases.

2. Automation, AI & CPQ Depth

Compares workflow execution caps, custom object limits, AI credit allocations (1k vs 5k/mo), conversation intelligence, predictive churn scoring, and whether CPQ, asset registry, and white-label tenant portals are native or paid add-ons.

3. Integration Interconnection & Queue Risk

Analyzes pre-built connectors for AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi, QuickBooks, and legacy SOAP middleware. Verifies delay guarantees that absorb labor costs if third-party API interconnection exceeds baseline timelines.

4. Security, Compliance & Tariff Arbitrage

Evaluates SSO/MFA, AES-256 with CMK/HSM, SOC2, HIPAA BAA, 90-day vs 7-year WORM audit vault, 99.9% vs 99.95% SLA, offline continuity (72-hr sync buffer), and metered SMS/VoIP/storage overages that impact 3-year TCO.
