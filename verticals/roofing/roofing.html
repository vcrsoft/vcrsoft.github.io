Why This Structure Changes the Game

1. Apples-to-Apples Feature Breakdown

Normalizes conflicting vendor terminologies across membrane types (e.g., TPO thickness mil ratings), insulation R-values, and fastener patterns without arbitrary scoring. For instance, it directly pairs Proposal A's 45-mil TPO membrane specification against Proposal B's 60-mil fleeceback system with tapered insulation packages, helping you evaluate engineering specs side-by-side.

2. Exposing Hidden Price Differences & Scope Gaps

Unpacks price deltas driven by hidden line items. Rather than leaving you to guess why competing contractors vary, the report highlights tangible additions: deck replacement unit pricing, saturated insulation tear-out allowances, perimeter metal flashing details, and manufacturer NDL (No Dollar Limit) warranty requirements versus standard contractor guarantees.

3. Operational Disruption & Scheduling Impact

Translates fine print into business realities. Proposal A may restrict tear-off timing or omit debris hauling logistics that risk clogging tenant parking or drainage, whereas Proposal B accounts for safety protocols, crane staging permissions, and weather-tight daily tie-offs to protect your building interior.

4. Hard-Hitting Questions for Vendors

Equips you with targeted questions to level the playing field before signing. By analyzing omissions in both source documents—such as Proposal B's missing exact fastener density metrics and Proposal A's lack of explicit core cut test provisions—the tool forces contractors to close loopholes and clarify missing scope upfront.
