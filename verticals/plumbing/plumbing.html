Why This Structure Changes the Game

1. Apples-to-Apples Material & Spec Breakdown

Normalizes conflicting contractor terminology across piping materials (e.g., Type L copper vs. schedule 80 PVC/CPVC), valve assemblies, and backflow preventer specifications without arbitrary scoring. For instance, it directly aligns Proposal A's cast iron waste stack replacement specs against Proposal B's trenchless pipe-lining approach, allowing you to weigh structural longevity against initial disruption.

2. Exposing Hidden Price Differences & Scope Gaps

Unpacks price deltas driven by hidden line items. Rather than leaving you to guess why competing bids vary, the report highlights tangible additions: concrete floor penetration patching, grease interceptor capacity ratings (GPM), municipal plan review fees, and emergency after-hours service provisions.

3. Operational Disruption & Facility Downtime

Translates fine print into business realities. Proposal A may require complete water main shutdowns during business hours without temporary bypass provisions, whereas Proposal B accounts for freeze-plugs, phased off-hours tie-ins, and temporary commercial restroom trailers to maintain business continuity.

4. Hard-Hitting Questions for Contractors

Equips you with targeted questions to level the playing field before signing. By analyzing omissions in both source documents—such as Proposal B's missing seismic hanger details and Proposal A's lack of explicit slab X-ray/scanning provisions—the tool forces contractors to close loopholes upfront.
