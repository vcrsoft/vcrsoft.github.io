1. Structural Longevity vs Value Engineering Risk

Compares belled 40ft vs straight 48ft piers, 5000 vs 6500 psi with corrosion inhibitor & fibers, Grade 80 vs Grade 60 – critical for 100-year luxury asset in expansive Gulf Coast clay.
