Why This Structure Changes the Game

1. Multi-Bid Normalization (3+ Proposals)

Comparing three or more complex electrical bids manually often leads to confusion over differing panel ratings (e.g., 1200A vs 1600A services), conduit types (EMT vs Rigid Galvanized), and transformer efficiencies. This tool normalizes multiple vendor proposals into a single coherent structure.

2. Uncovering Hidden Exclusions & Allowances

Instantly flags discrepancies in ancillary requirements such as utility coordination fees, concrete transformer pads, arc-flash safety labeling, and temporary generator feeds required during main service cutovers.

3. Business Continuity & Power Interruption Planning

Evaluates how each contractor handles scheduled electrical shutdowns. Compares bids that require daytime facility downtime against those offering phased after-hours cutovers and temporary backup generation.

4. Cross-Examining Contractors with Targeted Questions

Synthesizes gaps across all three proposals to generate precise, hard-hitting questions you can send to vendors before signing, ensuring all hidden costs and code compliance items are addressed upfront.
